Q1. What is UPI?
UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a real-time payment system that allows you to send and receive money instantly between bank accounts using a mobile number or UPI ID, with no need to share bank details.
Q2. How do I link my bank account?
Go to "Link Bank Account" in your dashboard, select your bank, verify your mobile number, and set or enter your UPI PIN. Once verified, your account will be linked automatically.
Q3. Is UPI safe for online transactions?
Yes. UPI is secure, and every transaction is encrypted and verified by your bank and NPCI. You must enter your UPI PIN for each payment, ensuring only you can authorize it.
Q4. How can I send money using UPI?
Go to "Send Money", enter the receiver's UPI ID or mobile number, or scan their QR code. Add the amount, confirm using your UPI PIN, and the money will be transferred instantly.
Q5. How can I request money?
Open "Request Money", enter the sender's UPI ID or mobile number, add the amount, and send the request. The sender will receive a notification to approve the payment.
